# Mobile App Redistribution

CrewCmd's mobile client is intended to support internal and self-hosted deployments.

## Permitted

- building and distributing a branded mobile client for one organization's own employees or contractors
- distributing a mobile client that connects to that organization's own self-hosted CrewCmd deployment
- publishing private enterprise builds through Apple Business Manager, MDM, Managed Google Play, or equivalent channels
- modifying icons, names, colors, bundle identifiers, and default server settings for internal deployment

## Not Granted

- any trademark or logo rights for Axislabs or CrewCmd without separate permission
- the right to use the mobile client to offer a competing hosted CrewCmd-style SaaS
- the right to imply official Axislabs endorsement of a fork or white-labeled distribution

## Practical Interpretation

If an organization self-hosts CrewCmd and distributes a mobile client only for access to that environment, that is within the intended use of the project.

If a third party republishes the client as part of a competing hosted offering, that falls outside the intended redistribution model.

This document is a practical policy note, not a replacement for the repository license.
